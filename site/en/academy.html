Body & CPR

See whatyour CPR does.

The manikin, the heart, the brain, the blood flow and the lungs respond to every compression and every breath. At a glance, you see why the quality of the CPR changes everything.

CPR dashboard

Measure,play, improve.

Depth, rate, recoil: the CPR dashboard shows the live measurement and a score out of 100, with a timed 2-minute test. Game targets: 5 to 6 cm, 100 to 120 compressions per minute, full recoil.

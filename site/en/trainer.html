Trainer Mode

One iPadfor the whole session.

On iPad, or in the browser with the web panel. Vital signs, rhythms, failures, scenarios: the trainer sets everything live, and the learners' monitors react at once.

  • On iPad

    The trainer station is an app: one iPad is enough to run the whole session.

  • In the browser

    The vitalxtrainer.app web panel runs the session with nothing to install.

  • No Internet between iPads

    iPads talk over a local link: no Internet, no server.

01

Session & patient

Local link, no Internet

The trainer creates the session and shares a 4-character code. Trainees’ iPads join it directly, with no Internet and no server: the Session link sheet shows the link quality.

Live vital signs

HR, SpO₂, RR, EtCO₂, temperature, NIBP or invasive pressure: each value is set with a slider or in steps. The left column holds glucose, lactate, haemoglobin, SpCO and CO.

Make the patient evolve

The TEND button drifts HR, SpO₂, RR, EtCO₂ and NIBP towards targets: start delay, duration, then one tap to launch the trend. The patient deteriorates or recovers on their own.

02

ECG & cardiac arrest

ECG rhythms

Dozens of rhythms in one tap (sinus, STEMI, AF, VF, AV block, torsades, pacemaker…), with their variants: STEMI territory, block type. The editor adjusts waves, ST and intervals on all 12 leads.

Unplugged cables and sensors

ECG cable, SpO₂ sensor, capnography, defibrillation pads and each of the 10 ECG electrodes: plug them in when the trainee places them. An unplugged electrode drops the leads that depend on it.

Cardiac arrest, shock and AED

Arrest + asystole or arrest + VF + shock in one tap, pads placed or not. In automatic analysis, a shock is advised only if SHOCK is on and the rhythm is shockable.

03

CPR & ventilation

CPR with sensor feedback

A Bluetooth sensor placed on the manikin measures every compression: rate, depth, recoil. The CPR dashboard shows the live measurement and a score out of 100, with a timed 2-minute challenge.

Ventilation

The VENT tab sets the ventilated patient: spontaneous breathing, compliance and resistance, leak, obstruction, oxygen, power and failures. Clinical scenarios apply in one tap.

04

Scenarios & assessment

Ready-to-use scenarios

Pre-built patients in one tap, or timed scenarios: a timeline of events that changes vital signs, rhythm, sensors and documents at T+mm:ss. A library by audience (PSE 1, PSE 2, paramedic, physician) and an editor to build your own.

Assessment and debriefing

The EVAL drawer offers grids (PSE 1, PSE 2, Flash assessment, or your own): achieved, partial, missed. Score, verdict and timer are computed for you; the sheet exports to PDF for the debrief.

05

Media Center

Media Center: patient file and lab

The patient file (identity, allergies, lifestyle, conditions, medications) and the lab report are filled in from the iPad: choose a panel (normal, STEMI / ACS, sepsis…), adjust, send.

Blood gases, X-rays, urine dipstick

An arterial or venous sample the trainee analyses themselves (result 60 s after the cartridge starts), X-ray images sorted by category, a 10-test urine dipstick or a clinical panel.

Media Center

The patient file,at a swipe.

Screenshots of the Media Center panel on the DEFIGARD HD-7 (SCHILLER), with no trainer iPad connected.

  • ECG: the archive

    The 12-lead ECGs printed from the device are archived and can be reopened in the panel.

  • History: the session log

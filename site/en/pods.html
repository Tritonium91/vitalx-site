What it is

A compact monitor,controlled remotely.

The VitalX POD'S housing holds the monitor iPhone and its accessories — blood-pressure cuff, SpO₂/SpCO cable, AED pad — to recreate a realistic workstation in a format that fits in a pouch. The trainer controls the vital signs and the case progression from an iPad, an iPhone or the web trainer mode.

Real-time synchronisation over Wi-Fi or tethering, with a session code: several POD'S units can follow the same scenario.

On screen

What the trainee sees.

  • VITALS mode

    Heart rate, SpO₂, SpCO and NIBP, with the mean arterial pressure calculated.

  • BIO mode

    Blood glucose, ketones, lactate and haemoglobin, displayed as on a field meter.

  • SpO₂ waveform

    Dynamic plethysmography, normal or degraded trace depending on the scenario.

  • NIBP button

    Inflation sound, result displayed at the end of the measurement, just like on a real blood pressure monitor.

  • Simplified AED

    Analysis, simulated shock and CPR metronome, right on the iPhone.

  • Synchronisation

    Wi-Fi or 4G/5G tethering, session code, several monitors on the same case.
